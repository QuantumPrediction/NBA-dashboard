Method

From data to discipline

01

A committee, not a guru

Seven independent statistical models, retrained on seasons of game history, each estimate every game. No single opinion decides.

02

The market must agree

A signal is issued only when the committee speaks with one voice, with conviction, for the side the market also favours.

03

A record that cannot be rewritten

Flat paper stakes. Counted picks require verified pre-tip publication and matching official-result settlement records. Missing closing prices stay missing.

Net profit: −1.32 stake amounts

Net profit is measured in stake amounts; bankroll return divides profit by an initial 100-stake bankroll.
